CHANGE: tone of writing
Target direction: {direction}
- "confident": assertive, active wording. Use words such as "proven", "successfully",
  "strong", "expert" ONLY where they describe something that is already in the CV.
  Do not add first person if the original has none. Never add achievements, results,
  numbers, leadership or seniority.
- "hedged": cautious, modest wording, only for (a) how the person describes themselves
  and (b) what they are looking for. Examples: "active and purposeful" -> "fairly active",
  "looking for" -> "hoping to find". Never hedge what was done, achieved or used: tools,
  projects, roles, numbers, durations and results stay exactly as stated. Do not add doubts
  about quality ("might not have done it perfectly", "though I might be wrong"). Use first
  person ("I think", "I hope") only if the original already uses first person.
If the CV is mostly a list of terms, change only connecting words and short phrases and
add no content.
Do not touch education, career history or skills.
